About Me

I am an M.Eng. candidate in Biological & Biomedical Engineering (Biomanufacturing) at McGill University in Montreal. My undergraduate studies provided a foundation in molecular biology and relevant laboratory techniques. I view my graduate training as a bridge between this scientific foundation and industrial production, developing my understanding of how biological knowledge is translated into reliable manufacturing processes.

I bring a detail-oriented approach to protocol writing, data analysis and process troubleshooting. My industrial internship interests include manufacturing science and technology (MSAT), process validation, technology transfer and production roles in monoclonal antibody (mAb), mRNA vaccine and therapeutic protein manufacturing.
